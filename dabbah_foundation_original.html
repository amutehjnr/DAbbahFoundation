Who We Are

A Movement for Change in Nigeria's Communities

D'Abbah Foundation is a youth-focused non-governmental organization committed to preventing drug abuse and restoring hope among individuals and communities across Nigeria.

We work through education, community engagement, and support systems to create lasting impact and positive behavioral change — because we believe every young person deserves a chance to thrive.

🎓

🔗

🔭 Vision Statement

"A Nigeria where every young person grows up in a safe, drug-free environment, empowered to make healthy choices and live their fullest potential."

🎯 Mission Statement

"To break the chains of drug abuse by empowering individuals and communities through transformative education, compassionate support, and strong collaborations for healthier, drug-free lives."

🔥

The Phoenix Symbol

Why It Works

Our Holistic Approach

📚

Education

Targeted, age-appropriate education equipping young people with the knowledge and confidence to make drug-free choices before peer pressure sets in.

⏰

Early Intervention

Identifying and supporting at-risk youth and families before addiction takes hold, with evidence-based screening and counseling resources.

🌍

Community Involvement

Engaging schools, local leaders, religious institutions, and families as partners, because lasting change requires the whole community.

💪

Continuous Support

Ongoing recovery groups, mentorship, and follow-up ensure that healing isn't a one-time event but a sustained journey toward wholeness.
